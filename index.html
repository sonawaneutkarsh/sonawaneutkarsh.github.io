Research

Extracting adverse drug reaction reports from Reddit

Undergraduate research · Penn State · 2026–present

In progress

With Dr. Md Sultan Al Nahian, I’m studying how language models can identify reported drug–reaction relationships in Reddit discussions. The work compares direct prompting, retrieval-augmented generation, and extraction with verification against human annotations.

Evaluation focuses on precision, recall, F1, and error analysis.

Research plan

As an Undergraduate Research Assistant at Penn State (REU), I am investigating LLM-based detection of adverse drug reaction reports and extraction of suspected drug–reaction relationships. The research plan compares direct prompting, retrieval-augmented generation (RAG), and agent-based extraction and verification. Planned analysis includes normalizing drug and reaction terminology and comparing extracted relationships with PubMed literature.
